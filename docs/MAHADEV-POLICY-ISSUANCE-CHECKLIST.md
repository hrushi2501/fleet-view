# Mahadev FleetView policy issuance checklist

`/policies` and `/my/policies` are an internal operational notice for FleetView users. They are deliberately marked **ready for Mahadev approval**, not automatically issued law or a substitute for legal advice.

Before Mahadev publishes or requires acknowledgement of the policy, an authorised Mahadev HR/legal representative must:

1. Name a privacy/grievance contact and a contact method available to employees and contractors.
2. Approve the retention schedule for driver identity documents, GPS and telematics, vehicle and fuel records, incident evidence, safety records, finance/tax records, and backups.
3. Confirm the Mahadev–Pitbull agreement defines each party's data-processing duties, support access, confidentiality, subprocessors, incident notification, backup/return/deletion arrangements, and any cross-border processing.
4. Confirm the collection notice, operational purpose, and any required consent or other lawful basis for each data type and workflow.
5. Publish the approved policy and worker notice in languages suitable for affected users; record acknowledgement only if Mahadev defines the lawful process and evidence requirements.
6. Train supervisors on urgent incident escalation, correction requests, privacy complaints, lost-device handling, and access removal when a worker leaves.

The policy page already states these decisions visibly so users are never told that anonymous or unspecified contacts and retention periods are binding policy.
